# Surface: the app

The single surface. Mode: **Operate** — the visitor completes a task (make a
creature, understand how it was made). Shipped: this is the whole product.

## Direction contract

**THESIS.** The interface dimensions its own machinery. Every number the
generator solved is drawn on the creature that it shaped, so moving one control
visibly moves a different number. It refuses the category default: a near-black
chrome with a viewport left and a scrolling slider panel right, where the
architecture is invisible and the controls are knobs.

**OWN-WORLD.** A drafting sheet whose title-block grammar governs the whole
interface. Charcoal film `#0f171c`, hairline rules at 30–45% bone `#dbe4e8`,
zone letters down the edge and numbers along the foot. Every control group is a
ruled module with a stamped monospace tab (`A1 PLAN`, `B3 LIMBS`), packed two to
five across. No rounded corners, no shadows, no glow. Saira Condensed for
labels, Share Tech Mono for every number. Three accents with jobs, never
decoration: **amber `#f2b134`** is what the system solved, **vermilion
`#e2503a`** is what you chose, **cyan `#4e93ad`** is datum and grid.

**STORY.** Someone arrives with no introduction, sees a creature walking inside
a drawing sheet, drags one slider, and watches a dimension somewhere else redraw
because the generator re-solved it. They understand that nothing here was
authored.

**FIRST VIEWPORT.** Full-bleed sheet, 14px margin, single hairline frame. Mode
strip across the top ending in the vermilion RANDOMISE block, the only filled
control. Left rail 176px: three tabbed mutation modules, legend pinned at its
foot. Centre: the drawing field, creature at ~230px, cyan datum across its base,
amber dimensions with witness ticks and leaders out to the right margin. Right
rail 268px: five stamped spec modules over a title block of solved totals and
the vermilion ident. Nothing below a fold; a sheet does not scroll.

**FORM.** User-pinned fusion of my top-ranked grounded candidate (the
dimensioned engineering drawing) with the dealt challenger
`japanese-high-density`; the roll had assigned candidate 6, the taxonomic card
catalogue. A user pin beats the roll. Seed key `168cbf1e`.

**Raises carried in from the declined hand.** One consistent line language and a
real legend, after the transit diagram. Overlay state in line weight and dash
rather than hue, after the emission-line rail — the creature already owns every
colour. The triangle budget printed as a hard number, after the cassette j-card.

**Dimension rule.** Standing dimensions are title-block facts only — height,
width, triangles, joints, ident — always on, never moving. Every leader-line
dimension is contextual: it draws itself while you touch the control that
changes it, and fades when you let go. The sheet is quiet on arrival and
annotates itself as you work.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the
finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its
provenance.
